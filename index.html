Our Story

How it began

Rosalinda and Gerard met at Costco, where they worked together — and for Gerard, it was love at first sight. Not long after, they welcomed two dogs, Winter and Shadow, and bought a home together, where they built a life for many years. Winter and Shadow have since passed, but their love lives on in the family that followed: Samson and Spruce, and a bunny named Cinnabon. Rosalinda and Gerard cherish their animals and can't resist a good walk together.
